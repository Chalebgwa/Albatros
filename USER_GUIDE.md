# User Guide - Albatros AR Quiz

Welcome to Albatros, the AR quiz application that makes learning about world landmarks fun and engaging!

## Getting Started

### First Launch

When you first open the app, you'll see the Home screen with four main tabs at the bottom:

1. **AR Scan** 📷 - Scan landmarks or select from demo list
2. **Missions** 🎯 - View and complete challenges
3. **Badges** 🏆 - See your earned achievements
4. **Profile** 👤 - Track your progress and stats

### How to Play

#### 1. Discover a Landmark

**Demo Mode (No AR Required):**
1. Open the app and go to the **AR Scan** tab
2. Scroll through the list of famous landmarks
3. Tap on any landmark to start a quiz

**AR Mode (With AR Device):**
1. Point your camera at a famous landmark
2. Wait for the app to recognize it
3. The quiz will appear automatically

#### 2. Answer the Quiz

Once a landmark is detected:
1. Read the question carefully
2. Select one of the four answer options
3. See immediate feedback on your answer
4. Read the explanation to learn more
5. Earn points for correct answers!

#### 3. Earn Badges

Badges are awarded for various achievements:

- **First Discovery** - Find your first landmark
- **Explorer** - Discover 5 landmarks
- **3-Day Streak** - Complete quizzes for 3 consecutive days
- **Week Warrior** - Maintain a 7-day streak
- **Century Club** - Earn 100 total points
- **Quiz Master** - Earn 500 total points

Check the **Badges** tab to see all available badges and your progress.

#### 4. Complete Missions

Missions give you goals and reward you with bonus points:

**Daily Missions:**
- Discover 1 landmark today
- Answer 3 questions correctly today

**Weekly Missions:**
- Discover 3 landmarks this week
- Answer 10 questions correctly this week

**Special Missions:**
- Maintain a 7-day streak
- Discover specific landmarks

Visit the **Missions** tab to track your progress.

## Understanding Your Stats

### Profile Screen

Your profile shows important statistics:

- **Total Points** - Sum of all points earned
- **Current Streak** - Consecutive days of activity
- **Longest Streak** - Best streak achieved
- **Landmarks Discovered** - Number of unique landmarks found
- **Badges Earned** - Number of achievements unlocked

### Points System

- Each correct answer: **10 points**
- Mission completion: **5-50 points** (varies by mission)
- Bonus points for streaks and achievements

## Tips for Success

### Building Your Streak

1. Complete at least one quiz each day
2. Set a daily reminder to use the app
3. Start with easier landmarks if you're new
4. Read the explanations to learn more

### Maximizing Points

1. Answer questions carefully
2. Complete daily missions for bonus points
3. Maintain your streak for special badges
4. Try to discover all landmarks

### Earning Badges

1. Check the Badges tab regularly to see requirements
2. Focus on specific achievements one at a time
3. Some badges unlock automatically as you play
4. Streak badges require consecutive daily activity

## Settings & Options

### Reset Progress

If you want to start over:
1. Go to the **Profile** tab
2. Tap the settings icon (⚙️)
3. Select "Reset Progress"
4. Confirm the action

**Warning:** This will delete all your progress, badges, and missions permanently!

## Troubleshooting

### AR Not Working

**Problem:** Camera doesn't recognize landmarks

**Solutions:**
- Ensure good lighting conditions
- Hold camera steady
- Use demo mode to select landmarks manually
- Check camera permissions in device settings

### App Crashes

**Solutions:**
- Close and reopen the app
- Restart your device
- Ensure you have the latest version
- Clear app cache in device settings

### Missing Progress

**Problem:** Points or badges disappeared

**Solutions:**
- Close and reopen the app
- Check that you didn't accidentally reset progress
- Ensure device storage isn't full

## Privacy & Permissions

### Required Permissions

- **Camera**: To scan landmarks using AR
- **Location** (Optional): To suggest nearby landmarks

### Data Storage

All your progress is stored locally on your device:
- Points and streaks
- Earned badges
- Mission progress
- Landmark visit history

No data is shared with external servers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need AR to use the app?**
A: No! Demo mode lets you select landmarks from a list.

**Q: How often do missions reset?**
A: Daily missions reset every 24 hours, weekly missions reset every 7 days.

**Q: Can I earn the same badge twice?**
A: No, each badge can only be earned once.

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**
A: Your streak resets to 1, but you keep all your points and badges.

**Q: How many landmarks are there?**
A: Currently 5 landmarks, with more coming in future updates!

**Q: Can I play offline?**
A: Yes! All features work offline except for initial app setup.
